PsyMap is a minimalist, 100% offline emotional diary that helps you understand how you feel.

<b>Key features:</b>

▪ <b>Emotion wheel</b> — select base emotions or create your own
▪ <b>Intensity</b> — record the strength of each feeling (1 to 5)
▪ <b>Notes</b> — add annotations to each entry
▪ <b>History</b> — view, filter, and delete past entries
▪ <b>Statistics</b> — frequency charts, distribution, trends, days of the week, and time of day
▪ <b>Export PDF</b> — generate reports to share with your therapist
▪ <b>3 themes</b> — Dracula, Dark, and Light

<b>Privacy:</b> PsyMap does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays saved only on your device. No login, no cloud, no telemetry.

<b>100% free and open source (GPLv3).</b> Distributed without trackers, ads, or proprietary dependencies.
